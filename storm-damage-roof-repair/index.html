What we look for

Signs Your Roof Has
Storm Damage

Some damage is obvious. A lot of it isn't — that's why an inspection matters.

01

Hail Damage

Dented or bruised shingles, granule loss and dented metal flashing or vents.

02

Wind Damage

Lifted, curled or missing shingles, often concentrated along roof edges and ridges.

03

Water Intrusion

Leaks, ceiling stains or attic moisture that started after a storm passed through.
